CBT

/ˌsiː biː ˈtiː/ · Cognitive Behavioural Therapy

CBT means a practical, structured therapy that helps you notice unhelpful thought patterns and gently change how you respond to them.

What it can help with

Anxiety, low mood, panic, stress, phobias and habits of thinking that keep you stuck.

Talking Therapy

/ˈtɔːkɪŋ ˈθɛrəpi/ · Counselling & Psychotherapy

Talking therapy means a calm, confidential space to talk things through, make sense of difficult feelings, and find a clearer way forward.

What it can help with

Relationship difficulties, grief, life changes, low self-esteem and feeling overwhelmed or unheard.
